One row per objection raised, including the ones that lost. An objection that
produces no diff either was not understood or was not real; decide which, out
loud, in the Reason column.

Verdicts: `accepted` · `rejected` · `rejected for now` · `deferred` ·
`not understood — asked again`.

A `rejected` row must still name the condition under which the objection would
have won. That is the same tripwire as
[Topic 2](../02-rejected-alternatives/README.md), raised by someone else.

---

Paste this at the top of the RFC itself once the decision is made, and keep it
there permanently:

```markdown
**Status:** Accepted · <date>
**Decision:** <one sentence: what we are doing>
**Dissent on record:** <who disagreed with what, and why we proceeded anyway>
```

The dissent line is written so the dissenter would agree it is a fair statement
of their position. Show it to them before you commit it; that check costs one
message and is the difference between a record and a summary of who won.
