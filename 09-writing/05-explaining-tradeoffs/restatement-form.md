## Scoring

| | Result |
|---|---|
| Did they restate the **decision** correctly? | |
| Did they restate the **cost** correctly? | |
| Did they use any of your nouns? (using them is a miss, not a hit) | |
| Could they produce an argument against? | |

**If they could not produce an argument against**, the exercise has not run yet.
"Sounds good, go ahead" with no questions usually means they did not understand
the cost and are deferring to you — approval without buy-in, and you find out
the difference when those weeks appear on a status report.

**If they used your nouns**, they decoded your jargon rather than receiving a
converted version, and the conversion step was never exercised. Check who you
sent it to: an engineering manager who already agrees with you measures nothing.

## What you changed as a result

One line. If nothing changed, say that too, and say why — a restatement that
comes back perfect on the first attempt is either a very good page or the wrong
reader.
